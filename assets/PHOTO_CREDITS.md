**Note on the Corporate Catering photo:** it shows identifiable people (a real
conference buffet line, not a Maroon Door event) — the crop is biased toward the
chafing dishes to de-emphasize faces, but it's still someone else's event. Treat
this one as first in line to replace with real photography or a faceless
alternative.

**Note on the Umm Al Quwain delivery-zone photo:** similarly shows real people
(a public beach at sunset, silhouetted, not identifiable close-up, but still
bystanders in someone else's photo) — same caveat as Corporate Catering above.

**Note on the Our Story trust photo:** used deliberately as a hands-only visual
metaphor for care/trust, not a claim that these are Simak or Nazia's actual
hands — the whole point of this redesign is fixing a previous site that
captioned stock photos as if they were the real chef/owner, so this page never
does the same thing in the other direction. **Swapped once already**: the
first pick (same photographer, "Two children's hands press cookie cutters into
rolled dough") was replaced after a visual review caught that it read as a
kids'-baking-activity photo, not a fit for a premium catering brand's founder
story, despite being an honest hands-only crop. This strawberries photo was
chosen instead for tone (adult hands, healthy/fresh produce, matches the
"Healthy, Fresh, and Made Just for You" tagline) even though, like its
predecessor, it shows one person's two hands rather than two people's —
Wikimedia's CC-licensed catalog doesn't have a ready "two adult siblings'
hands, faces excluded" shot, and an anonymous single-person hands photo stays
on the right side of the honesty line this file is drawing, where a
recognizable stranger's face captioned as if it were a founder would not.
